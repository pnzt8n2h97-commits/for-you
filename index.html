        Put it at the top of Stremio

        Sign in to your Stremio account and this add-on moves to the top of your add-ons, so its rows come first on your home screen. If it isn't installed yet, this installs it too.

        Signed up with Facebook or another login?

        Use your Stremio auth key instead. Open web.stremio.com signed in, open the browser console, and run JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem("profile")).auth.key. Paste what it shows here. Treat it like a password.

        Your email and password go straight from this page to Stremio (api.strem.io). This site never receives or saves them, and the page signs out of that Stremio session when it's done.
